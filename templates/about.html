WHAT IS IKS?

Indian Knowledge Systems

Indian Knowledge Systems refers to the diverse traditions through which knowledge has been developed, preserved, transmitted and practiced across the Indian subcontinent.

These traditions include areas such as mathematics, astronomy, medicine, philosophy, linguistics, architecture, agriculture, arts and other forms of knowledge.

The purpose of this website is to provide an accessible starting point for exploring these traditions and understanding their historical and intellectual contexts.

WHY STUDY IKS?

Connecting Knowledge, History & Culture

Studying Indian Knowledge Systems provides an opportunity to examine how different communities developed, preserved and transmitted knowledge across generations.

It also encourages us to look at historical knowledge traditions through multiple disciplines and contexts.

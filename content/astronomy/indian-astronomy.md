# Indian Astronomy

Indian astronomical traditions include systematic observation of celestial bodies and methods for understanding their movements.

## Observing the Sky

Historical astronomical traditions involved observing the Sun, Moon, planets and stars.

These observations supported the development of systems for measuring and understanding time.

## Calendars

Astronomical knowledge was closely connected with calendars.

Observations of celestial cycles helped develop methods for organizing time and dates.

## Astronomy and Mathematics

Mathematical methods played an important role in astronomical calculations.

This relationship between mathematics and astronomy became an important feature of several Indian scholarly traditions.

## Historical Importance

Indian astronomy provides an important area for studying the development and transmission of scientific and mathematical knowledge.

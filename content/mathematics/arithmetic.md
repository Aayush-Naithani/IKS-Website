# Arithmetic

Arithmetic is one of the fundamental branches of mathematics. It deals with numbers, quantities, and the basic operations used to perform calculations.

## Introduction

Arithmetic forms the foundation of mathematical thinking. The four fundamental operations are:

- Addition
- Subtraction
- Multiplication
- Division

These operations are used in everyday calculations as well as in more advanced areas of mathematics.

---

## Addition

Addition is the process of combining two or more quantities.

For example:

**5 + 3 = 8**

Here, 5 and 3 are added together to obtain 8.

### Example

If a person has 10 apples and receives 5 more apples:

**10 + 5 = 15**

The person now has 15 apples.

---

## Subtraction

Subtraction is used to find the difference between two quantities.

For example:

**10 - 4 = 6**

### Example

If there are 20 books and 7 books are removed:

**20 - 7 = 13**

There are 13 books remaining.

---

## Multiplication

Multiplication can be understood as repeated addition.

For example:

**4 × 3 = 12**

This can also be represented as:

**4 + 4 + 4 = 12**

### Example

If there are 5 boxes containing 10 items each:

**5 × 10 = 50**

There are 50 items in total.

---

## Division

Division involves distributing a quantity into equal groups.

For example:

**20 ÷ 5 = 4**

This means that 20 can be divided into 5 equal groups of 4.

### Example

If 24 students are divided into 6 equal groups:

**24 ÷ 6 = 4**

Each group contains 4 students.

Arithmetic has played an important role in the development of mathematical knowledge in India.

Numerical calculations were important in practical activities and in scholarly disciplines such as astronomy, trade, measurement, and calendar calculations.

Indian mathematical traditions also contributed to developments in numerical notation and calculation methods.

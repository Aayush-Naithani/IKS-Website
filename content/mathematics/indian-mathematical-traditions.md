# Indian Mathematical Traditions

Indian mathematical traditions developed across arithmetic, geometry, algebra and other areas of mathematical reasoning.

## Mathematics in India

Mathematical knowledge developed through different periods and intellectual traditions.

It was often connected with astronomy, calendrical calculations and practical applications.

## Aryabhata

Aryabhata was an important historical mathematician and astronomer.

His work contributed to mathematical and astronomical traditions in India.

## Numerical Traditions

Indian mathematical history includes important developments in numerical notation and calculation.

These traditions later became part of wider mathematical history through processes of transmission and exchange.

## Mathematics and Astronomy

Mathematics played an important role in astronomical calculations.

The relationship between mathematics and astronomy is therefore an important area when studying historical Indian knowledge traditions.

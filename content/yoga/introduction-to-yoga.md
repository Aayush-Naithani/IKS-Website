# Introduction to Yoga

Yoga encompasses a range of physical, mental and philosophical practices that developed within Indian traditions.

## What is Yoga?

Yoga is a diverse knowledge tradition that includes practices concerning the body, mind, attention and philosophical inquiry.

## Major Practices

Some practices associated with Yoga include:

- Asana
- Pranayama
- Meditation
- Ethical practices
- Philosophical study

## Yoga and Philosophy

Yoga is not limited to physical exercise.

Its historical traditions also contain philosophical ideas concerning consciousness, discipline, knowledge and human experience.

## Yoga Today

Yoga is practiced around the world today.

However, studying its historical development provides a broader understanding of Yoga beyond its contemporary physical practice.

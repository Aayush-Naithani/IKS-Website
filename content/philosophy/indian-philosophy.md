# Indian Philosophical Traditions

Indian philosophical traditions contain diverse approaches to questions concerning knowledge, reality, consciousness and existence.

## Philosophical Inquiry

Philosophical traditions examine fundamental questions about human experience and the nature of reality.

## Diverse Schools

Indian philosophy includes multiple schools and intellectual traditions.

These schools developed different approaches to questions concerning knowledge, perception, reality and liberation.

## Knowledge and Reality

Questions about what can be known and how knowledge is obtained are important themes within Indian philosophical traditions.

## Why Study Indian Philosophy?

Indian philosophy provides an extensive intellectual tradition for examining fundamental questions concerning human existence, knowledge and reality.

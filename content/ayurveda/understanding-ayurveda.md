# Understanding Ayurveda

Ayurveda is a traditional Indian knowledge tradition concerned with health, wellbeing and approaches to maintaining balance in life.

## What is Ayurveda?

Ayurveda includes a broad range of traditional approaches related to health, lifestyle, diet and wellbeing.

Its knowledge has been transmitted through texts, teachers and practical traditions over long periods of time.

## Key Ideas

Some areas associated with Ayurvedic traditions include:

- Health and wellbeing
- Diet and lifestyle
- Herbal knowledge
- Traditional approaches to healthcare

## Ayurveda as a Knowledge Tradition

Ayurveda can be studied as part of the broader Indian Knowledge Systems framework.

Its historical development provides insight into how knowledge concerning health and wellbeing was documented, transmitted and practiced.

## Why Study Ayurveda?

Studying Ayurveda helps us understand an important part of India's intellectual and cultural history.

It also provides an opportunity to examine how traditional knowledge systems approached questions concerning health, lifestyle and wellbeing.

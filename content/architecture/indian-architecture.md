# Indian Architectural Traditions

Indian architectural traditions include knowledge concerning structures, proportions, spatial organization and design.

## Architecture as Knowledge

Architecture involves more than constructing buildings.

Traditional architectural knowledge can include ideas concerning space, proportions, materials and organization.

## Temple Architecture

Temple architecture represents an important part of India's architectural and cultural heritage.

Different regions developed distinctive architectural forms and styles.

## Spatial Design

Traditional approaches to architecture often considered the relationship between buildings, spaces and their surroundings.

## Preserving Heritage

Studying traditional architecture helps document and understand India's diverse built heritage.

Preservation also allows future generations to study historical construction and design traditions.
